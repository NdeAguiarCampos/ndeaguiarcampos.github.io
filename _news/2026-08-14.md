Between 4 and 15 August 2026, I had the pleasure to co-teach [Ecological Research Methods](https://handbook.jcu.edu.au/subject/2025/bz5230) to undergraduate and postgraduate students from James Cook University. The subject was taught by Prof Lucas Cernusak in the Daintree Rainforest Observatory and had both me and Dr Jordy Groffen as tutors.

This intensive 2-week subject was quite a busy period for us all, but especially the students. They had to formulate hypotheses, collect the data to address them, run statistical analyses and present their progress over four oral presentations! The range of topics was quite diverse: pollination, crypsis, decomposition, avian behaviour and plant functional traits. 

It was incredibly rewarding to witness the scientific and intellectual development of the students over those two weeks!
